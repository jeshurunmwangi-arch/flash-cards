Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay?
You'll be redirected to PesaPal's secure checkout where you can pay via M-Pesa, Visa, Mastercard, or PayPal. Your payment is processed securely — we never store your card details.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes — cancel your subscription at any time with no penalty. You'll keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period.
What happens to my cards if I cancel?
Your cards are stored in your browser's local storage, so they stay on your device regardless of your plan. If you downgrade to Free, you simply won't be able to add more cards beyond the 10-card limit.
Is there a student discount?
We're working on a student plan! Reach out to us on WhatsApp and we'll sort you out with a discounted rate in the meantime.
