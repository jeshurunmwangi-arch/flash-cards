Get Started in 3 Steps

No sign-up required. Create your first flash card in under a minute.

01

Create a Card

Pick a subject you're learning and write a question about something you want to remember. Keep it focused — one concept per card works best.

✏️
02

Study & Recall

Go through your cards and try to answer each question from memory before flipping to check. This active recall is what makes flash cards so effective.

🧠
03

Review & Repeat

Keep studying until you've mastered every card. Delete ones you know, keep the ones you're still working on, and build up your deck over time.
